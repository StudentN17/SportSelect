About SportSelect

SportSelect is a prototype sports equipment catalogue designed for beginners and recreational athletes. The website focuses on making products easier to discover through clear categories, simple descriptions and an organized page structure.

The project will later include SEO improvements such as keyword mapping, internal linking, breadcrumbs, canonical checks and structured data.

This is an academic prototype. No payments or real transactions are processed.
